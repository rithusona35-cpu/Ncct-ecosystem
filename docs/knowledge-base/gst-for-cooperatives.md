# GST Compliance and Taxation for Cooperative Societies

## Applicability of GST to Cooperative Societies
Under the Indian Goods and Services Tax (GST) framework, a registered cooperative society is recognized as a taxable "person" under Section 2(84) of the CGST Act. While cooperative principles emphasize non-profit mutual services among members, transactions involving supply of goods or commercial services may attract GST liability unless specifically exempted by statutory notifications.

## Key Exemptions for Cooperatives
Certain critical activities carried out by cooperative societies enjoy statutory exemptions:

1. **Agricultural Produce Storage & Handling**: Warehousing services, cold storage, and handling of unmanufactured agricultural produce (such as paddy, wheat, pulses, and raw cotton) are exempt from GST.
2. **Rural Credit and Banking Operations**: Extension of loans, advances, or deposits where consideration is represented by interest or discount is exempt from GST under standard financial services provisions.
3. **Milk Producers' Cooperative Societies**: Supply of fresh unprocessed milk, curd, and natural dairy products by dairy cooperatives directly to consumers is generally zero-rated or exempt.
4. **Housing Society Maintenance**: Maintenance charges collected by cooperative housing societies up to INR 7,500 per member per month for sourcing common amenities are exempt from GST.

## Taxable Supplies and GST Rates
Cooperative societies that engage in trading or value-added processing are liable to register and charge GST:

- **Fertilizers & Chemicals**: Standard agricultural chemical fertilizers attract 5% GST; chemical pesticides attract 18% GST.
- **Agricultural Implements**: Manually operated agricultural equipment enjoys nil or low rates (5%), while powered tractors and mechanized harvesters attract 12% to 18% GST.
- **Consultancy & Business Services**: Management charges, agency commissions, and rental income from commercial shop front properties attract 18% GST.

## Invoicing, Input Tax Credit (ITC), and Return Filing
Registered cooperatives must comply with standard GST procedural norms:
- **Tax Invoices**: Must issue sequential GST-compliant tax invoices showing HSN/SAC codes, society GSTIN, and tax breakup (CGST + SGST or IGST).
- **Input Tax Credit (ITC)**: Taxes paid on purchase of taxable inventory or capital goods used in business can be offset against outward tax liabilities, subject to Section 16 & 17 restrictions.
- **Filing Deadlines**: Monthly returns including GSTR-1 (outward supplies by 11th of each month) and GSTR-3B (summary return and tax payment by 20th of each month) must be filed timely via the GST Common Portal to avoid late fees and interest penalties.
