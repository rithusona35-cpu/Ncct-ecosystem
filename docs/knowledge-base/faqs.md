# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) - NCCT Ecosystem

## General & Program Questions

### What is the National Council for Cooperative Training (NCCT)?
The National Council for Cooperative Training (NCCT) is an autonomous organization under the Ministry of Cooperation, Government of India. NCCT is responsible for planning, promoting, and coordinating cooperative training and capacity building across India through the apex Vaikunth Mehta National Institute of Cooperative Management (VAMNICOM), Pune, alongside 5 Regional Institutes of Cooperative Management (RICMs) and 14 Institutes of Cooperative Management (ICMs).

### Who is eligible to enroll in NCCT training programmes?
Eligibility varies by curriculum level. Diploma and certification programmes are open to personnel employed in cooperative departments, cooperative banks, PACS, marketing federations, and fresh agricultural/management graduates seeking professional careers in the cooperative sector.

## Learning Management System (LMS) & Attendance

### What is the minimum attendance requirement to receive course certification?
Trainees must maintain a minimum of 75% verified attendance across scheduled sessions to remain eligible for final assessments and course completion certificates. Attendance is logged digitally via biometric kiosks and synchronized daily.

### How do I access module learning materials and video lectures?
Enrolled trainees can log in to the NCCT LMS portal using their registered credentials. Under the "My Courses" tab, click on any active module to stream video lectures, download PDF reference guides, and complete self-paced review quizzes.

## Digital Certificates & Skill Passport

### How does the NCCT Digital Skill Passport work?
The Dynamic Skill Passport is a tamper-evident digital credential summarizing a trainee's verified competencies across core domains (such as Cooperative Accounting, PACS ERP, GST Compliance, and Cooperative Law). Skill levels are dynamically calculated (Levels 1 through 5) based on continuous assessment results, project assignments, and formal evaluations.

### How can employers verify an NCCT certificate?
Every certificate issued by NCCT features a unique certificate identifier and an embedded QR code. Employers and verification agencies can scan the QR code using any smartphone camera or enter the certificate code at `/verify` to inspect cryptographic validity, issuing institute details, and candidate grade in real time.

### What should I do if my course completion status does not update?
Ensure you have completed all mandatory content items in each module and achieved a passing score on the module evaluation quiz. If completion remains below 100%, contact your assigned institute batch coordinator or trainer.
