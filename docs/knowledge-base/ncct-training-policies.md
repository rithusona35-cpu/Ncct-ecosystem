# NCCT Institutional Training Policies and Guidelines

## Institutional Mandate
The National Council for Cooperative Training (NCCT) formulates and regulates national academic standards, curriculum frameworks, and assessment guidelines for all cooperative education and training institutions operating across the Indian Union. Its primary objective is to modernize the human capital managing cooperative societies, agricultural credit networks, and cooperative urban/rural financial systems.

## Training Delivery Framework
NCCT institutes implement a blended pedagogy combining theoretical foundations with hands-on computer lab training:

1. **Classroom Instruction**: Focuses on cooperative law, management principles, financial statements, and national cooperative policies.
2. **Practical Lab Simulation**: Hands-on operational simulations utilizing computerized PACS ERP software, Tally Prime, and Core Banking Solution testbeds.
3. **Field Placement & Cooperative Study Visits**: Trainees participate in on-site field visits to successful Primary Agricultural Credit Societies, dairy cooperative plants, sugar factories, and multi-state cooperative federations.

## Trainee Code of Conduct and Assessment Standards
To maintain high academic rigor and institutional discipline:

- **Attendance Norms**: A strict 75% attendance threshold is enforced for all certificate-granting courses. Trainees falling below 75% attendance without verified medical excuse are barred from appearing in the end-term assessment.
- **Continuous Comprehensive Evaluation (CCE)**: Assessment is structured across internal tests (40% weightage), lab practicals (20% weightage), and final comprehensive theory evaluation (40% weightage).
- **Passing Criteria**: Trainees must score a minimum aggregate of 50% marks to qualify for course certification. Scores above 70% earn a "Distinction" endorsement on the official certificate.
- **Academic Integrity**: Plagiarism in project submissions, proxy attendance at biometric kiosks, or examination malpractice incurs immediate disqualification and de-registration from the national portal.

## Trainer Accreditation & Quality Assurance
Faculty members across VAMNICOM, RICMs, and ICMs undergo regular national training-of-trainers (ToT) programs. Course curriculum is reviewed annually in consultation with NABARD, national cooperative federations, and state cooperative departments to align training with modern fintech, agricultural market trends, and statutory revisions.
