# Primary Agricultural Credit Societies (PACS) Explained

## What is PACS?
A **Primary Agricultural Credit Society (PACS)** is the foundational village-level pillar of the short-term cooperative credit structure in India. PACS operates directly at the grassroots level with individual farmers, rural artisans, and agricultural laborers as its primary voting members. Under the three-tier cooperative credit architecture, PACS forms the base tier, federated at the district level into District Central Cooperative Banks (DCCBs), which are in turn federated at the state level into State Cooperative Banks (StCBs).

## Key Objectives and Functions
The core mandate of a Primary Agricultural Credit Society is to provide timely, affordable credit and vital agricultural inputs to rural farmers. Major functions include:

1. **Short-Term Crop Loans**: Extending seasonal agricultural credit to member farmers under schemes like the Kisan Credit Card (KCC) for purchasing seeds, fertilizers, pesticides, and meeting harvesting expenses.
2. **Medium-Term Credit**: Financing investments in agricultural machinery, irrigation pump sets, animal husbandry, and farm modernization.
3. **Input Distribution**: Supplying certified seeds, fertilizers, and plant protection chemicals directly to farmers at regulated, subsidized rates.
4. **Storage and Warehousing**: Providing local godown storage facilities so farmers can store produce and avoid distress sales immediately following harvest.
5. **Consumer Goods & Distribution**: Acting as fair-price shops and Public Distribution System (PDS) outlets in remote rural habitations.

## Governance and Member Structure
PACS functions under strict democratic cooperative principles. Every member holds one vote regardless of the number of share units owned (*"One Member, One Vote"*). The general body of members elects a Managing Committee or Board of Directors, which appoints a full-time Secretary or Chief Executive to oversee daily operations and financial accounting.

## National Digitization of PACS
Under the National Vision of *"Sahakar se Samriddhi"* (Prosperity through Cooperation) led by the Ministry of Cooperation, a nationwide initiative has been launched to computerize over 63,000 functional PACS. Digitization transitions PACS from manual ledgers to a cloud-based National Unified ERP software. This initiative links PACS directly with NABARD, DCCBs, and StCBs, ensuring financial transparency, automated audit trails, faster loan processing, and diversification into multi-service rural hubs (such as Common Service Centers, Farmer Producer Organizations, and decentralized grain storage).
